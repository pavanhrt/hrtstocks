## Known unresolved terminology and conflicts

- `AMAR`, `AKBAR`, `ANTHONY`, `CVECp`
- `BBNC-DN`, `BBNC-UP`, `BBC`, `AZ`, `BZ`, `P > 50`, `P < 50`
- PAPA intermediate Advanced Dow comparisons
- `UBBCF/UBBFC` and `LBBCF/LBBFC` spelling variants; internal canonical forms are `UBBCF` and `LBBCF`
- SMM 8% versus FOME 10% monthly-loss limit; provisional shared value is 8%
- FOME bearish-row `NP` conflict
- Subjective terms such as significant, reasonable, heavy, huge, major, shakeout, weapon, operator-driven, and clear pattern

If an unresolved item is required for classification, return `MANUAL_REVIEW` or `CONFLICT` rather than choosing the favorable interpretation.
